# v16.3 — Chief-of-Staff Preparation Intelligence

## UI polish
- Removed the Home subtitle: “Today, tomorrow, preparation context, and your action pressure in one view.”
- Made the sidebar “Last AI load” status brighter/whiter for dark sidebar readability.
- Added Copy buttons for Today and Tomorrow preparation summaries.

## Preparation intelligence
- Reworked Today/Tomorrow preparation summaries into Chief-of-Staff style guidance.
- Prep now emphasizes what to focus on, what to ask, risks/dependencies, action ownership, and decision closure.
- Per-meeting prep cards and full-prep popups now use the same executive-prep framing instead of raw mechanical context dumps.

## Chat intelligence
- Improved direct meeting title extraction for questions like: “What is the follow-up context and prep for Discuss Smart Attribute - E wallet || 25th May 26?”
- Direct/latest matching now prioritizes subject overlap and date/title relevance before broader semantic context.
- Reduced drift to unrelated meetings such as Commerz & ING when a direct meeting title is present.
- Updated answer prompt to produce Chief-of-Staff-style prep: Executive Prep, Follow-up Context, Questions to Ask, Watch-outs, Confidence.

## Why this matters
- Preparation and chat should now behave less like a transcript summarizer and more like an executive aide preparing the user for what deserves attention.
