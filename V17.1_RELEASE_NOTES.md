# v17.1 — Chief-of-Staff Chat Intelligence

## What changed

The chatbot now treats questions like "what should be my focus today?" as a Chief-of-Staff brief, not as a generic RAG answer.

It synthesizes across:
- Today's calendar meetings
- Open and overdue action items
- High-priority actions
- Recent notes and memory
- Active / at-risk / blocked threads
- Linked meeting context

## New behaviour

When the user asks:
- What should be my focus today?
- What needs my attention today?
- Give me a Chief-of-Staff brief
- What should I prioritize today?
- Prepare me for today

The assistant returns:
- Chief-of-Staff Brief
- Your Focus Today
- Meetings to Prepare For
- Decisions / Escalations Needed
- Risks to Watch
- Do Not Spend Time On

## Guardrails

The assistant is instructed to avoid generic advice like:
- "focus on open actions"
- "validate risks"
- "align stakeholders"
- "confirm next steps"

Every recommendation must cite concrete evidence: meeting name, action, thread, person, risk, decision, or context note.

## Fallback

If OpenAI fails, the deterministic fallback still builds a structured brief from meetings, actions, context and threads instead of failing silently.
