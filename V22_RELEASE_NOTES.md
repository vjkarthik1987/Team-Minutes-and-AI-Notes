# v22 — People + Org Intelligence

## Added
- People Intelligence page with ownership, blocker, follow-up and execution-load view.
- Person detail pages showing owned threads, collaboration threads, actions, signals and alias memory.
- Alias memory support, e.g. `Sreejith TG = Sreejith`.
- People signal refresh engine for ownership, blocker, follow-up and collaboration signals.
- Dedicated action item detail page with comments/updates, status editing, escalation and reassign controls.
- Thread metric chart options expanded from line/bar to line, bar, area, step and share-list style.
- Transcript AI Summary and Detailed Notes pages widened to use the full available content area.

## Why it matters
v22 starts turning meeting memory into an organisational execution graph: who owns what, who is blocked, who needs follow-up, and how thread metrics are trending.
